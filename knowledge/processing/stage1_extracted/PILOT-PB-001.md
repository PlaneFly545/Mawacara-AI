# Respectful Conduct in Public Places

## Definition
Visitors are expected to behave respectfully in public settings including roads, restaurants, shopping centers, attractions, and shared community spaces.

## Cultural context
Bali Governor Circular Letter No. 07 of 2025 requires respectful behavior in sacred areas, attractions, restaurants, shopping centers, roads, and other public places. It prohibits profane language, disrespectful behavior, disturbances, and aggressive conduct toward officials, local communities, or fellow tourists.

## Significance
Public conduct affects residents, workers, other visitors, and shared use of public space.

## Common situations
- Speaking with residents or service workers
- Disagreements about prices or services
- Moving through busy public areas
- Posting content about local people online

## Do
- Speak politely and remain calm when resolving disagreements
- Respect residents, workers, officials, and other visitors
- Follow posted instructions and directions from authorized personnel

## Don't
- Use abusive or threatening language
- Harass or intimidate others
- Create disturbances or act aggressively in public

## Misconceptions
- Being on holiday does not exempt visitors from Indonesian law.
- Not every disagreement is automatically a legal offence.

## Regional variations
- Specific conduct rules may apply at particular attractions, businesses, ceremonies, or customary village areas.

## Notes
Distinguish the circular's requirements from general etiquette. Do not infer penalties; refer to current law and competent authorities.
