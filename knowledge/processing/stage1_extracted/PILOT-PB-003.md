# Respectful Appearance and Photography

## Definition
Visitors should dress appropriately for the setting and avoid photography or public behavior that disrespects people, ceremonies, sacred places, or religious symbols.

## Cultural context
The 2025 Governor Circular asks foreign tourists to dress modestly and respectfully in sacred areas, attractions, public places, and activities in Bali. It prohibits indecent or inappropriate photographs, including nudity, when these desecrate sacred places, temples, sacred objects, or religious symbols.

## Significance
Clothing and photography can affect dignity, privacy, and the sanctity of a place or event.

## Common situations
- Taking photos of residents or performers
- Photographing ceremonies
- Visiting public attractions or sacred sites

## Do
- Dress respectfully and follow venue-specific clothing requirements
- Ask permission before close-up portraits of identifiable people
- Follow photography signs and organizer instructions

## Don't
- Take or publish indecent or nude photographs at sacred places
- Ignore venue or ceremony restrictions
- Assume public access means unrestricted photography

## Misconceptions
- The circular's dress guidance does not mean every venue has the same dress code.
- Photography rules may vary by location and event.

## Regional variations
- Temples, ceremonies, private businesses, and public events may each have their own requirements.

## Notes
Consent for portraits is a courtesy recommendation; the circular specifically addresses respectful dress and indecent photography at sacred places.
