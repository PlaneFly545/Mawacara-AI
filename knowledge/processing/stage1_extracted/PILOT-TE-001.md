# Driving Eligibility and Legal Vehicle Use

## Definition
Anyone driving in Bali must comply with Indonesian driving-licence requirements and use a legally operated vehicle appropriate for the journey.

## Cultural context
The 2025 Governor Circular requires foreign tourists operating vehicles to hold a valid national or international driving licence and use four-wheeled transport from legally registered rental businesses or associations. Kejati Bali's FAQ summarizes licence and vehicle-document expectations.

## Significance
A valid licence and legally operated vehicle help ensure drivers are authorized and vehicles can be identified by authorities.

## Common situations
- Renting a scooter or car
- Using a borrowed vehicle
- Being stopped at a traffic checkpoint

## Do
- Confirm your licence is valid for the vehicle class and recognized in Indonesia
- Carry required driving and vehicle documents
- Use a properly registered rental provider and check roadworthiness

## Don't
- Drive without the required valid licence
- Assume a home-country licence alone is sufficient
- Use a rental vehicle when its legal status is unclear

## Misconceptions
- Renting a scooter does not itself authorize someone to ride it.

## Regional variations
- Underlying driving rules are national; the Bali circular reiterates obligations for foreign tourists.

## Notes
For legal questions, refer users to Indonesian Police or the relevant licensing authority. Do not make individualized legal determinations.
